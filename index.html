The Mashwi Method

Slow Flame Perfection

Real taste takes patience. We marinate our fresh chicken overnight with crushed garlic and hand-picked Arabic spices. It is then cooked on slow flame steadily, locking in all natural juices so the meat stays soft while the outer skin develops that irresistible aroma.

1. Overnight Marination

Whole fresh chicken rested 12+ hours with crushed garlic, yogurt, and authentic Arabian spices to tenderize every fiber.

2. Slow Flame Roasting

Turned gently over steady low flame. No burning, no hurry—letting the chicken baste in its natural juices until golden.

3. The Grand Platter

Carved to order and served steaming hot alongside yellow Bukhari rice, crunchy potatoes, spicy Daqoos and garlic dip.
