Our Story

Authentic Heritage Cuisine

Chicken Mashwi specializes in slow-roasted, charcoal-grilled rotisserie chicken. Our chicken is marinated in traditional Arabian spices like sumac, smoked paprika, and cumin, then roasted over open flames to achieve a perfectly crispy, golden-brown skin.

The Mashwi Method

Charcoal-Grilled to Perfection

We don't cut corners. Every chicken is marinated overnight in our signature blend of sumac, smoked paprika, cumin, and secret Arabian spices. It is then slow-roasted over an open charcoal flame, locking in the juices while rendering the skin perfectly crisp and deeply flavorful.
